**Full description:**
UniSwap is the campus marketplace for UET Lahore students. Rent out the lab coat, calculator or drafting kit you are not using, or borrow what you need for a semester instead of buying it new.

• Verified students only: sign up with your @student.uet.edu.pk email
• Find items near you: listings are sorted by distance from your campus zone
• Rent or buy: request an item, the owner accepts, you meet on campus
• Handover and return records with photos protect both sides
• Ratings and trust scores for every student
• Chat with owners inside the app
• Campus feed for posts, lost and found, and notes
• Block and report tools keep the community safe

UniSwap never handles money. You settle rent and prices in person.
