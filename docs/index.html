UniSwap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

UniSwap is a campus marketplace app where students of the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Lahore rent, buy and sell items with each other. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. UniSwap was built as a university software engineering project.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we need it
University email address and passwordTo create your account and check you are a UET student (only @student.uet.edu.pk addresses can register). Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
Display name, department (optional), profile photo (optional), campus zoneTo show who you are to other students and to sort listings by distance from your zone.
Listings: title, description, price, deposit, pickup zone, photosTo show your items to other students.
Requests, handover and return records (notes and up to 2 photos), reviews and ratingsTo run rentals and sales, protect both sides in a dispute, and build trust scores.
Messages between you and another studentTo let you arrange pickups and ask questions.
Campus feed posts, photos, likes and commentsTo power the campus feed.
Reports and blocksTo keep the community safe and let moderators review problems.
Push notification tokenTo send you notifications about requests and messages.
Photos from your camera or galleryOnly the photos you choose to attach. The app asks permission first and does not read other photos.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, payment details, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or advertising tools.

2. Who can see your information

  • Other UniSwap students (signed-in users) can see your display name, department, profile photo, trust score, reviews, listings and feed posts.
  • Only you and the other person in a conversation can see your messages. Only the two people in a rental or sale can see its handover and return photos and notes.
  • Moderators (admins) can see reports, the content that was reported, and information needed to resolve a dispute.
  • Listing, feed and profile photos are stored in public storage and can be opened by anyone who has the exact link. Do not upload photos you do not want shared.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Services that process your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
  • An email service sends verification and password-reset emails.
  • Expo delivers push notifications to your phone.

These providers process data on our behalf and only to run the app.

4. Money

UniSwap never handles payments. Rent, deposits and prices are settled in person between students. We do not collect or store any payment information.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, listings, posts, comments, messages, reviews, notification settings and the files you uploaded are permanently deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, department, photo and zone in Profile → Settings.
  • Turn off notification types in Settings.
  • Block any user, and report content, from the “…” menu.
  • Delete your account and data in Settings → Delete my account. You must first finish or cancel any active requests or rentals.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by email (see Contact).

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to data is restricted by database rules so students can only read and change what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

UniSwap is for university students and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
